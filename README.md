# Artificial Intelligence Applications in Esophageal Dysphagia and Related Esophageal Disorders

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.22558079.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22558079)

This public repository contains the editorially revised submission materials for a JBI-guided scoping review reported according to PRISMA-ScR.

## Project status

The screening, eligibility, consensus, evidence charting, PRISMA accounting, and bibliography have been completed and ratified by the review team. This repository preserves the supporting materials for the scoping review. The manuscript has not yet completed journal peer review and should not be described as a peer-reviewed publication.

Verified review totals:

- 2,664 records identified
- 1,003 duplicate records removed manually
- 1,661 records screened
- 180 reports sought for retrieval
- 9 reports not retrieved
- 171 reports assessed for eligibility
- 139 full-text reports excluded
- 32 reports included, representing 31 studies

## Repository structure

- `manuscript/` — edited manuscript
- `protocol/` — retrospectively registered protocol and amendment log
- `supplement/` — clean supplementary materials, including complete search strategies
- `reporting/` — PRISMA-ScR checklist and flow diagram

## Methodological framework

The review uses age-neutral Population–Concept–Context eligibility criteria. The age-criterion amendment was dated 21 August 2026 and retained in the audit trail. Six documented sources were searched on 6 August 2026: PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase, Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, IEEE Xplore, and ClinicalTrials.gov.

## Important limitations

- The exact Web of Science constituent indexes were not recorded.
- No documented formal peer review of the search strategies occurred.
- Nine reports were not retrieved and are reported separately from eligibility exclusions.
- No formal critical appraisal was conducted.

## Authors
